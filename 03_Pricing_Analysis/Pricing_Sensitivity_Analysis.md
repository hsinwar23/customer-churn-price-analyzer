# Pricing Sensitivity Analysis

## 1. Objective

The objective of this analysis is to explore the relationship between subscription pricing and customer cancellation behaviour.

Understanding how customers respond to pricing can help businesses evaluate their pricing strategies and identify potential customer retention challenges.

## 2. Key Areas of Analysis

### Pricing and Customer Behaviour

Examine the relationship between:

- Monthly subscription charges.
- Total customer charges.
- Subscription duration.
- Customer cancellation behaviour.

### Customer Price Sensitivity

Explore whether customers with different subscription characteristics show different cancellation patterns in relation to pricing.

## 3. Key Business Questions

1. Do customers with higher monthly charges show different churn patterns?
2. Are certain customer segments more sensitive to pricing?
3. Does subscription duration influence the relationship between pricing and churn?
4. Which customer groups may require pricing-related retention attention?
5. What pricing strategies could help improve customer retention?

## 4. Potential Business Opportunities

Businesses could explore:

- Reviewing pricing structures across customer segments.
- Introducing suitable subscription plans for different customer needs.
- Evaluating loyalty-based pricing incentives.
- Offering targeted retention discounts where commercially justified.
- Improving the perceived value of subscription offerings.

## 5. Strategic Business Recommendations

Pricing decisions should balance customer affordability, perceived value, retention and business profitability.

The analysis can help businesses identify potential pricing concerns and explore opportunities to improve customer retention without unnecessarily reducing revenue.

## 6. Business Relevance

Understanding customer price sensitivity can support better pricing decisions, stronger customer relationships and sustainable revenue growth.

The analysis identifies associations between pricing variables and churn. It does not independently establish that price changes cause customer cancellations.
