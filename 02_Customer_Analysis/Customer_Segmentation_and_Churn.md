# Customer Segmentation & Churn Analysis

## 1. Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand customer behaviour and identify patterns associated with subscription cancellations.

The focus is on understanding which customer groups may require greater attention from a customer retention perspective.

## 2. Customer Segmentation

Customers can be examined across different characteristics, depending on the information available in the dataset:

- Subscription type
- Customer tenure
- Monthly charges
- Contract duration
- Payment method

Segmenting customers helps businesses understand differences in customer behaviour and identify groups that may need specific retention strategies.

## 3. Churn Behaviour Analysis

The analysis focuses on examining:

- Overall customer cancellation rates.
- Differences in churn across customer segments.
- Subscription characteristics associated with cancellations.
- Potential customer retention challenges.

## 4. Key Business Questions

1. Which customer segments show higher churn?
2. Do customers with shorter subscription tenure show different cancellation patterns?
3. Are cancellation patterns different across contract types?
4. Which customer groups should receive greater retention attention?

## 5. Potential Business Opportunities

Businesses could explore:

- Targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer segments.
- Loyalty initiatives for valuable existing customers.
- Improved onboarding for newer subscribers.
- Contract-based retention incentives.
- More personalized customer engagement.

## 6. Business Relevance

Understanding customer segments helps businesses move away from generic retention campaigns towards more focused customer engagement strategies.

## 7. Payment Method Analysis

### Churn Rates by Payment Method

The analysis shows differences in observed churn across payment methods:

| Payment Method | Customers Churned | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bank transfer (automatic) | 258 | 16.71% |
| Credit card (automatic) | 232 | 15.22% |
| Electronic check | 1,071 | 45.29% |
| Mailed check | 308 | 19.11% |

### Key Observation

Customers using electronic checks have the highest observed churn rate at **45.29%**, compared with 15.22% for customers using automatic credit card payments.

### Business Interpretation

The difference suggests that payment method may be a useful factor when identifying customer groups that need additional retention attention.

### Strategic Opportunity

Businesses could investigate whether payment convenience, billing experience, or payment-related friction contributes to the higher churn observed among electronic-check customers. They could also test whether encouraging convenient automatic payment options improves the customer experience.

### Analytical Limitation

These results show an association between payment method and churn. They do not establish that payment method causes customers to cancel their subscriptions.


The objective is to support better customer retention, improve customer experience, and protect recurring revenue.
