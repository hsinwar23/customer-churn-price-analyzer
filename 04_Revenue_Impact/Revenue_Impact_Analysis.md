# Revenue Impact Analysis

## 1. Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand how customer cancellations can affect recurring revenue and long-term business growth.

The focus is on examining the potential financial implications of customer churn and identifying opportunities to protect existing revenue.

## 2. Key Areas of Analysis

### Revenue Exposure

Explore:

- Monthly revenue associated with customer subscriptions.
- Revenue associated with customers who cancel.
- Differences in subscription value across customer segments.
- Potential recurring revenue exposure from customer churn.

### Customer Value

Understand the importance of:

- Monthly customer contribution.
- Customer subscription duration.
- Long-term customer relationships.
- Customer retention and lifetime value.

## 3. Key Business Questions

1. How much recurring revenue is associated with customers who cancel?
2. Which customer segments contribute more to subscription revenue?
3. Are higher-value customers also at risk of cancellation?
4. How could customer retention help protect recurring revenue?
5. Which retention opportunities could provide the greatest business value?

## 4. Potential Business Opportunities

Businesses could explore:

- Prioritizing retention efforts for valuable customer segments.
- Identifying recurring revenue exposure.
- Improving retention among long-term customers.
- Evaluating the financial value of retention initiatives.
- Focusing resources on commercially important customer groups.

## 5. Strategic Business Implications

Customer churn is not only a customer experience issue but also a revenue management challenge.

Understanding the financial implications of customer cancellations can help businesses prioritize retention initiatives and make better resource allocation decisions.

## 6. Business Relevance

Revenue impact analysis connects customer behaviour with business performance.

It helps organizations evaluate customer retention from a financial perspective and identify opportunities to support sustainable revenue growth.
