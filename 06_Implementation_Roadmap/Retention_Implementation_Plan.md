# Customer Retention Implementation Roadmap

## 1. Objective

The objective of this roadmap is to outline how customer retention and pricing-related recommendations could be implemented and monitored.

The roadmap focuses on practical execution, business priorities, and measurable outcomes.

## 2. Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Customer Analysis

- Review customer churn patterns.
- Identify customer segments with relatively higher cancellation rates.
- Understand customer characteristics and subscription behaviour.
- Identify potential revenue exposure.

### Phase 2: Strategy Development

- Prioritize customer segments for retention initiatives.
- Evaluate pricing-related opportunities.
- Develop targeted customer engagement strategies.
- Define measurable retention objectives.

### Phase 3: Pilot Initiatives

- Test targeted retention campaigns with selected customer segments.
- Evaluate onboarding improvement initiatives.
- Test suitable customer incentives where commercially justified.
- Compare outcomes against an appropriate baseline or control group.

### Phase 4: Performance Evaluation

- Monitor customer churn and retention rates.
- Evaluate revenue retained through initiatives.
- Assess campaign performance.
- Review customer response to pricing initiatives.

### Phase 5: Optimization and Scaling

- Identify initiatives that demonstrate positive results.
- Refine underperforming strategies.
- Allocate resources towards effective retention initiatives.
- Expand successful initiatives across relevant customer segments.

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following metrics can be used to evaluate performance:

- Customer churn rate.
- Customer retention rate.
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
- Retention campaign conversion rate.
- Revenue retained through retention initiatives.

## 4. Business Considerations

Implementation should consider:

- Customer experience.
- Marketing and retention costs.
- Pricing and profitability.
- Operational resources.
- Long-term revenue sustainability.

## 5. Expected Outcomes

The proposed roadmap aims to support:

- More structured retention planning.
- Better prioritization of business initiatives.
- Improved measurement of retention performance.
- More informed pricing and customer engagement decisions.
- Sustainable recurring revenue growth.

Actual outcomes should be evaluated after implementation using measurable business results.

## 6. Data-Driven Implementation Priorities

Based on the completed customer churn analysis, the following priorities are recommended:

| Priority | Customer Group | Observed Churn Rate |
|---|---|---:|
| High | Customers with less than 12 months tenure | 48.28% |
| High | Electronic check customers | 45.29% |
| High | Month-to-month contract customers | 42.71% |
| Medium | Customers in the $70–$80 monthly charge range | 39.82% |

### Suggested Action Sequence

1. Identify customers belonging to the high-churn segments.
2. Investigate the reasons behind their cancellations.
3. Design targeted retention initiatives.
4. Test the initiatives with selected customer groups.
5. Monitor churn rates and recurring revenue to evaluate effectiveness.

### Measurement and Review

Review the following indicators regularly:

- Overall customer churn rate.
- Churn rate by customer segment.
- Monthly recurring revenue.
- Customer retention campaign performance.

These priorities are based on observed associations in the dataset. Their effectiveness must be evaluated through actual implementation and measurement.
