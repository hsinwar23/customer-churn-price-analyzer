# Business Problem & Objectives

## 1. Business Context

Subscription-based businesses depend on retaining existing customers
to maintain recurring revenue and achieve sustainable growth.

Customer cancellations can reduce revenue, increase customer
acquisition costs, and negatively affect long-term customer value.

Understanding why customers leave and how pricing relates to
customer behaviour can help businesses make better retention
and pricing decisions.

## 2. Problem Statement

The key business challenge is to identify customers who are more
likely to cancel their subscriptions and understand the factors
associated with their cancellation behaviour.

The analysis focuses on customer characteristics, subscription
patterns, and pricing-related factors to identify potential
retention opportunities.

## 3. Key Business Questions

1. What customer characteristics are associated with higher churn?
2. Which customer groups may require greater retention attention?
3. Is there an observable relationship between pricing and churn?
4. How can businesses prioritize customers for retention initiatives?
5. What strategies could help reduce customer cancellations?

## 4. Project Objectives

- Analyze customer behaviour and subscription cancellation patterns.
- Identify customer segments with relatively higher churn.
- Explore potential pricing-related churn patterns.
- Identify opportunities for targeted customer retention.
- Develop practical recommendations to support recurring revenue growth.

## 5. Expected Business Value

The project aims to provide insights that can support:

- More focused customer retention strategies.
- Better understanding of customer pricing concerns.
- Improved prioritization of retention efforts.
- More informed pricing decisions.
- Sustainable customer and revenue growth.

## 6. Project Scope

This project focuses on customer churn analysis, pricing-related
patterns, customer risk identification, and business recommendations.

The analysis is intended to support business decision-making.
It does not claim to establish causal relationships between
pricing changes and customer cancellations.
