# Customer Churn & Price Sensitivity Analyzer

## Project Overview

A business-focused customer analytics project designed to understand subscription churn, identify high-risk customer segments, examine pricing patterns, and evaluate recurring revenue associated with customer cancellations.

The project uses customer data analysis in Microsoft Excel to generate actionable insights that support customer retention, pricing strategy, and business growth decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Understand customer churn patterns.
- Identify customer segments with higher observed churn.
- Examine the relationship between monthly charges and customer cancellations.
- Evaluate contract and payment-method differences.
- Estimate monthly charges associated with churned customers.
- Develop practical customer retention recommendations.

## Key Project Findings

| Business Metric | Finding |

| Highest-Churn Monthly Charge Range | $70–$80 |
| Churn Rate in $70–$80 Range | 39.82% |

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Churn Analysis
- Calculated overall customer churn.
- Compared retained and churned customers.
- Examined customer tenure patterns.

### 2. Contract Analysis
- Compared churn across month-to-month, one-year, and two-year contracts.
- Identified customer groups with higher observed cancellation rates.

### 3. Pricing Analysis
- Grouped customers by monthly subscription charges.
- Calculated churn rates across pricing ranges.
- Created a chart to visualize pricing-related churn patterns.

### 4. Payment Method Analysis
- Compared churn across different payment methods.
- Identified electronic-check customers as the segment with the highest observed churn rate.

### 5. Revenue Impact Analysis
- Calculated monthly charges associated with churned customers.
- Estimated average monthly charges per churned customer.

## Strategic Recommendations

- Strengthen onboarding and early customer engagement.
- Investigate retention opportunities for month-to-month customers.
- Review the billing experience of electronic-check customers.
- Evaluate subscription value in higher-churn pricing segments.
- Develop targeted retention initiatives.
- Monitor recurring revenue and customer churn regularly.

## Dataset & Analytical Limitations

This project uses a sample telecom customer dataset containing 7,043 customer records.

The findings describe observed relationships in the dataset. They do not establish that pricing, payment method, or contract type directly causes customer churn.

The monthly charges associated with churned customers represent an estimate of recurring subscription value, not verified historical revenue loss.

The recommendations are proposed business actions and have not been tested through live retention campaigns.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how customer data can be translated into practical business insights. By combining churn analysis, customer segmentation, pricing analysis, and revenue assessment, the project supports more informed customer retention and growth decisions.
